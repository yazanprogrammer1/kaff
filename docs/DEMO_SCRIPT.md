# Demo script: 45 seconds, two phones

**Setup:** two phones on a dark table, front cameras up, brightness high, Do Not Disturb on.
Phone A (sender) on the left, phone B (receiver) on the right. Pre-pair once off camera so the
digit check is already done (show it in shot 3 only as an insert). Shoot on a phone gimbal at
eye level, 4K/60 for slow-motion inserts. Soft key light from the front so hands track well.

| # | Time | Shot | On screen | Audio / caption |
|---|------|------|-----------|-----------------|
| 1 | 0–4 s | Wide top-down, both phones on Home | Breathing hero hand, «ارفع إيدك.. وانقل» | Caption: *What if sending a photo felt like handing it over?* |
| 2 | 4–9 s | Close on A, thumb taps «ابدأ الإرسال», picks a sunset photo | Photo tile floats above the camera portal | Soft UI tick |
| 3 | 9–13 s | Insert: both screens side by side | Same digits **4721** on both; B taps «الرمز مطابق» | Caption: *Paired with a 4-digit check* |
| 4 | 13–19 s | Medium on A; hand closes into a **fist** over the phone | Skeleton glows, tile lifts with particle trail, pill «تم مسك الصورة», 5 s ring starts | Haptic thump (sound design), caption: *Grab it.* |
| 5 | 19–23 s | Whip pan / match cut from A to B | Offer card «صورة واردة من Pixel 8» with thumbnail and shield row | — |
| 6 | 23–30 s | Medium on B; hand **opens** facing the phone | Hold arc fills, rings pulse outward, progress bar to 100 % | Caption: *Open your palm.* |
| 7 | 30–36 s | Close on B (slow-mo 50 %) | Photo materializes blur→sharp, check badge, «وصلت الصورة», «٤٢ ميجا/ث» | Success chime |

**Tips**
- Do a real take of shots 4–7 in one continuous run at least once; viewers trust uncut proof.
- If tracking hesitates, raise the light or sensitivity in Settings. Do not fake the gesture.
- Keep a short "honest" card ready for comments: no background camera, Android only, and the
  gesture is a trigger, not identity.
